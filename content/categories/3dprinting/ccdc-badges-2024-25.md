Title: Programmatically generating and rendering 3D printed ID card holders
Date: 2025-03-27
Category: 3D Printing
Status: draft

For CCDC, we need to have two different ways to identify many of the
participants: On the one hand, we need to be able to show their role
in the competition: student, coach, volunteer, guest, staff (and, if
so, what staff role). On the other hand, for those who are playing 
in-character roles, we need to show their role in the _in-game world_.
The solution we've used for the last two years is to produce conventional
corporate style ID cards for simulated company employees (including the
student competitors), and to use a 3D printed credential to represent the
competition role for the participant.

We have some interesting requirements for these. We want them to be fun
and related to the theme of the competition. They need to be variations of
a similar design that are nevertheless clearly distinguishable. I print
these in multi-color on my X1 and A1 Mini, so I put text on the bottom
and need to be able to generate a lot of variations of them for
each role and team number. And, finally, because it's an important game
credential, we need to have visual renderings of them in our briefing
materials and written documentation.

Enter OpenSCAD.
