# Summary

My 2001 Jaguar S-Type 4.0 V8 is for sale in Tulsa, with about 75,000 miles. Asking $6,000 OBO, cash only. I bought it last August specifically to take it on a 5,400-mile road trip up through eastern Canada and back through Maine, and it did the trip in impeccable style. Now that the trip is over, I'm ready to sell. I'm the third owner; the previous one kept the car for 22 years in the San Francisco Bay Area.

# The car

It's a fun car to drive: comfortable and fast. All electronics work. AC, heater, and automatic climate control work great. Sunroof, cruise control, power windows, locks, and mirrors. Driver side electronic seat controls with memory function. HomeLink garage door buttons in the visor and an auto-dimming rearview mirror with compass. The cassette player and 6-CD changer also work great. The factory navigation works, technically, though the maps are on CDs that are over 25 years old and the two it comes with don't include Oklahoma. Still fun.

Comes with 2 keys and 2 fobs, all working great. The owner's manual is still in its original leather folio in the glove box. There's a Garmin Mini Dash Cam in it that I'm willing to factory reset and sell with the car.

Sold as-is. The dashboard has a small crack near the windshield. There are minor scuffs in the leather, and the speaker cover in the rear left door has a dent in it. The third brake light is a little loose, but it works fine.

You're welcome to come take it for a test drive, and a pre-purchase inspection is welcome, too. The Meineke at 11th and Yale has worked on it twice and done a fine job, but your own mechanic is welcome to check it out, too. I'm selling it because it's time to free up space, and it was only ever supposed to be a summer car for a road trip. Time for someone new to enjoy it.
